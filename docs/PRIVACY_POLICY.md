# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-27

Gym Rest Timer is an offline workout timer app. It helps you track active sets, rest countdowns, and stopwatch sessions on your device.

## Data collection

Gym Rest Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, cloud sync, accounts, or third-party tracking services.

## Data stored on your device

Gym Rest Timer stores rest profiles locally on your device, including profile names and rest durations. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

You can remove the app's local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android system settings.

## Permissions

Gym Rest Timer requests only permissions needed for timer functionality:

- Notifications: used to show the foreground timer/stopwatch notification and controls.
- Foreground service: used to keep an active timer or stopwatch running while the app is not in the foreground.
- Vibration: used for short countdown alerts near the end of a rest period.

These permissions are not used to collect personal data.

## Children

Gym Rest Timer is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.
